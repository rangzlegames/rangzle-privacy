🎨 Rangzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Simple summary: Rangzle does not collect any personal data from users. We only use Google AdMob for showing ads, which may collect some data as per Google's policy.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Rangzle ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile game Rangzle (the "App") available on Google Play Store.

2. Information We Collect

Rangzle does NOT collect any personal information such as:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or any files from your device
  • Any account or login information

The only data stored on your device is your game progress (current level and score), which is saved locally on your phone and is never sent to us or any server.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rangzle uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain data to show relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • App usage data

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device settings:
Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. Children's Privacy

Rangzle is a puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app does not contain any inappropriate content.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, your privacy is fully protected. Your game progress is stored only on your local device.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. AdMob Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
